General Information

This website was created to help me learn HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

You can change the theme, accent color, title, font, read random facts, and play mini games.

This project is a practice space to get better at coding step-by-step.

Features

  • Switch between Dark, Light, and Neon themes.
  • Change the accent color used on buttons and highlights.
  • Toggle between different title styles.
  • Cycle between different fonts for the page.
  • Show random facts.
  • Play two mini games: Click Race & Jump Runner.
  • Track best Click Race score and best Jump Runner time.

How to Use This Site

  1. Use the Theme dropdown to switch between Dark, Light, and Neon.
  2. Click Change accent color or press B to cycle colors.
  3. Click Change title or press T to switch title styles.
  4. Click Change font or press F to cycle between fonts.
  5. Scroll down to see random facts and play the mini games.
  6. In Click Race, click the button as fast as you can for 5 seconds.
  7. In Jump Runner, press the spacebar to jump and avoid the obstacle. You have 3 lives, try to survive as long as possible.

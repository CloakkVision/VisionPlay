General Information

This website was created to help me learn HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

You can change the theme, accent color, title, font, read random facts, and play mini games.

This project is a practice space to get better at coding step-by-step.

Features

  • Switch between Dark, Light, and Neon themes.
  • Change the accent color used on buttons and highlights.
  • Toggle between different title styles.
  • Cycle between different fonts for the page.
  • View random facts in multiple categories (world, animals, space, games, tech, history).
  • Play three mini games: Click Race, Jump Runner, and Reaction Test.
  • Choose Click Race time: 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 seconds or a custom time.
  • Play Jump Runner in Endless or Goal (250m) mode, with lives based on difficulty.
  • Test your reaction in Classic, Average, or Hard modes.
  • Track best scores in the Stats section and reset them.
  • Best scores and settings are saved locally so they stay after refresh.

How to Use This Site

  1. Use the Theme dropdown to switch between Dark, Light, and Neon.
  2. Click Change accent color or press B to cycle colors.
  3. Click Change title or press T to switch title styles.
  4. Click Change font or press F to cycle fonts.
  5. Use the Random Fact buttons to see facts about the world, animals, space, games, tech, or history.
  6. In Click Race, choose a time (preset or custom), press Start, and click as fast as you can.
  7. In Jump Runner, choose a mode and difficulty, press Start, and use spacebar or click to jump.
  8. In Reaction Test, choose a mode, press Start, and try to react as fast as possible.
